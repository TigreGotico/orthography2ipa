## Overview

> Sena (Chisena/Cisena, Nuclear Sena, Guthrie N.44), Bantu language spoken along the lower Zambezi valley in Mozambique (Sofala/Zambezia provinces) and adjoining Malawi. Five vowel qualities a/e/i/o/u.

The spec is at the [research](../specs/quality_tiers.md#research) tier, which asks for a cited description, with stress and a measured gold. No board row scores it yet, so the measured gold of that tier is still missing. It cites 2 works, from 2024 to 2025. 1 of them is a Wikipedia article. [Sources](#sources) lists them.
