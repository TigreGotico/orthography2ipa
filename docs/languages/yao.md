> Yao (Chiyao/Ciyao, Guthrie P.21), Bantu language spoken across southern Malawi, northern Mozambique and southern Tanzania. Standard (Malawian) orthography: five vowel qualities a/ɛ/i/ɔ/u with a length contrast optionally marked with a macron since plain vowel letters do not distinguish length.

The spec is at the [research](../specs/quality_tiers.md#research) tier, which asks for a cited description, with stress and a measured gold.
