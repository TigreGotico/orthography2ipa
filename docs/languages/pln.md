## Overview

The notes of the spec file open with these words:

> Palenquero (Lengua ri Palenge), the Spanish-lexifier creole of San Basilio de Palenque, Bolívar, Colombia (~3,500 speakers, the only surviving Spanish creole of Latin America). Kikongo (Bantu) substrate. KEY FEATURES: (1) FIVE-VOWEL SYSTEM /a e i o u/, Caribbean-Spanish-type.

The spec is at the [research](../specs/quality_tiers.md#research) tier, which asks for a cited description, with stress and a measured gold. No board row scores it yet, so the measured gold of that tier is still missing. It cites 3 works, from 2003 to 2013. [Sources](#sources) lists them. The spec file writes all 40 spellings of its letter table itself.

## Sources

The spec cites 3 works. 2 of them have a DOI or a URL. The spec file holds a note on what each work supports.

1. <a id="src-apics2013pln"></a>Schwegler, Armin (APiCS survey chapter) (2013). *Palenquero structure dataset (Survey chapter 48)*. Atlas of Pidgin and Creole Language Structures Online (APiCS), eds. Michaelis, Maurer, Haspelmath & Huber. Cited: Vowel inventory /i e a o u/ (Caribbean-Spanish-type), consonant inventory /p t k b d ɡ ʝ f m n ɲ tʃ s x l ɾ r/, word-initial prenasalized stops [mb nd ŋɡ] as African-substrate diagnostic (ngota, ndulo, mbala), seseo (no /θ/) and yeismo (/ʝ/, no /ʎ/) inferable from the inventory, 'one phoneme / one letter' modern orthography (Palenke, kumé). [apics-online.info](https://apics-online.info/surveys/48)
2. <a id="src-lipski2012"></a>Lipski, John M. (2012). *The 'new' Palenquero: revitalization and re-creolization*. In: Colombian Varieties of Spanish, eds. File-Muriel & Orozco, Iberoamericana/Vervuert, pp. 21-42. Cited: pp. 24-36: genuine glossed example sentences, TAM particles ta (imperfective), taba/-ba (past/imperfect), a (perfective/completive), asé (habitual), pronouns i (1sg), bo (2sg), ele (3sg), suto (1pl), ané (3pl), enú (2pl), postposed negation nu, preposed plural marker ma, copula jue, relativizer lo ke, the etymological c/qu transcription used for the gold register. [personal.psu.edu](https://personal.psu.edu/jml34/Palenquero-UniverSOS.pdf)
3. <a id="src-schwegler_morton2003"></a>Schwegler, Armin & Morton, Thomas (2003). *Vernacular Spanish in a microcosm: Kateyano in El Palenque de San Basilio (Colombia)*. Revista Internacional de Lingüística Iberoamericana 1(1), pp. 97-159. Cited: Contrast between Palenquero (creole) and local Kateyano (village Spanish), the prenasalized stops occur in the creole but not the local Spanish, intervocalic /b d ɡ/ lenition to [β ð ɣ], acute accent marks stressed/high-tone syllables.
