## Overview

> Phák-fa-sṳ (白話字, 'vernacular writing'), the Presbyterian-missionary Latin romanization for Hakka developed after 1847 (Basel Mission: Hamberg, Lechler, MacIver's 1905 Shantou dictionary is its first published form), still in active church and community use and the source script of the 1924 Hakka New Testament.
