## Overview

The notes of the spec file open with these words:

> Gen (Gɛn, Gɛ̃gbe, Mina, Guin, Popo), a Gbe (Kwa) language of the Maritime Region of southeastern Togo and adjoining Benin, mutually intelligible with and sometimes classed as a dialect of Ewe. Same Africa-Alphabet-derived Latin orthography convention as Ewe: ɖ ƒ ɣ ŋ ɔ ɛ ʋ plus digraphs gb kp ny ts.

The spec is at the [research](../specs/quality_tiers.md#research) tier, which asks for a cited description, with stress and a measured gold. No board row scores it yet, so the measured gold of that tier is still missing. It cites 2 works, from 2024 to 2026. 1 of them is a Wikipedia article. [Sources](#sources) lists them.
