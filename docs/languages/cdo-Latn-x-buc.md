## Overview

The notes of the spec file open with these words:

> Bàng-uâ-cê (平話字, BUC, 'Foochow Romanized'), the mid-19th-century missionary Latin romanization for the Fuzhou dialect of Eastern Min (Mindong), developed by American Methodist and Church of England missionaries (M. C. White's initial scheme, standardized by Maclay, Stewart, and Hartwell in the 1890s).

The spec is at the [research](../specs/quality_tiers.md#research) tier, which asks for a cited description, with stress and a measured gold.
